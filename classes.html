I specialize in four unique dance forms — each with its own personality, rhythm, and soul.

Dance Form

Contemporary

Contemporary dance is where emotion meets movement. It blends elements of ballet, modern dance, and lyrical expression to create a fluid, deeply personal art form. In my classes, you'll learn to tell stories through your body — connecting breath, music, and motion in a way that feels completely natural.

Floor work & improvisation techniques
Emotional expression & storytelling
Flexibility & body control training
Suitable for all skill levels
Join Contemporary
Dance Form

BollyHop

BollyHop is the electrifying fusion of Bollywood energy and Hip Hop swagger. It's for those who love the drama and expression of Bollywood combined with the sharp, powerful moves of urban Hip Hop. High energy, catchy beats, and pure fun — this is the style that makes you feel like a star on stage.

Bollywood + Hip Hop fusion choreography
Grooves, popping & locking basics
Stage performance techniques
High energy cardio-style workout
Join BollyHop
Dance Form

Salsa

Salsa is the dance of passion and connection. Originating from Latin America, it's a partner dance driven by infectious rhythms, quick footwork, and smooth body movement. Whether you're dancing solo or with a partner, Salsa teaches you timing, musicality, and the art of leading and following with confidence.

Basic steps, turns & partner work
Timing & musicality training
Social dance floor etiquette
Solo shines & footwork patterns
Join Salsa
Dance Form

Bachata

Bachata is grace personified. This Dominican dance form combines smooth body waves, intimate partner connection, and beautiful musicality into a dance that's both sensual and elegant. It's perfect for those who want a slower, more connected partner dance experience that still has incredible depth and expression.

Basic steps & body movement
Sensual & modern Bachata styles
Partner connection techniques
Body waves & isolations
Join Bachata

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining my dance classes.

Do I need prior dance experience?

Not at all! My classes welcome complete beginners. I start from the basics and gradually build your skills. Whether you've never danced before or you're looking to improve, there's a place for you.

How do I book a class?

The easiest way is to message me on WhatsApp! Just click the WhatsApp button on this page, tell me which dance style you're interested in, and I'll help you find the right batch and timing.

What age groups do you teach?

I teach students of all ages — from teenagers to adults. Dance has no age limit! Each class is adapted to the skill level and comfort of the students.

How are online classes conducted?

Online classes are conducted via video call in a small batch of 2 students. This ensures maximum attention and real-time corrections. All you need is a phone/laptop, a small space to move, and good internet.

Can I try a demo class before joining?

Absolutely! I offer trial sessions so you can experience the teaching style and decide which dance form suits you best. Message me on WhatsApp to schedule a demo.

What should I wear to class?

Wear comfortable clothes that allow free movement — track pants, leggings, or shorts with a t-shirt work great. For footwear, dance shoes or clean sneakers are recommended. Bare feet work well for Contemporary.

Ready to Start Your Dance Journey?

Pick your style, choose your format, and let's get moving. Message me on WhatsApp to book your first class!
