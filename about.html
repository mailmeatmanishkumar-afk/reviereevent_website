My Story

From Student to Master Trainer

My name is Manish Kumar, and dance has been my life's calling. My journey began at the prestigious M.D.S Dance Station in Patna, where I trained under the legendary choreographer Minku Kumar. Those foundational years shaped not just my technique, but my entire philosophy of teaching dance.

Over the past 7+ years, I've trained 150+ students across multiple dance forms — from the fluid elegance of Contemporary to the high-energy fusion of BollyHop, the passionate rhythms of Salsa, and the graceful intimacy of Bachata.

I believe dance is not just an art form — it's a language that transcends words. My goal is to help every student find their voice through movement, regardless of their skill level or background.

Skill Building

Mastered 4 Dance Forms
