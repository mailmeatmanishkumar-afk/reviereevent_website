Founder & Lead Director

Transforming Visions into Extraordinary Experiences

Welcome to Reviere Events. Led by founder and owner Manish Kumar, with over 7+ years of hands-on experience managing 50+ large-scale events across Patna and Bihar, we have built a reputation for delivering extraordinary, stress-free celebrations.

From high-stakes corporate summits and university cultural festivals to intimate private celebrations and grand wedding galas, Manish brings creative vision, meticulous planning, and sharp on-ground leadership to every single event produced under the Reviere Events banner.

By handling venue logistics, lighting production, decoration styling, catering, and vendor alignment under a single point of contact, we allow hosts to be true guests at their own events.
